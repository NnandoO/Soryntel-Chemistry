# Referencias Científicas — Módulo 12: Elements Module

1. Outotec / Outokumpu Research Oy — *HSC Chemistry 6 Manual, Chapter 24: Elements Module* (`24 Elements Module.txt`) y archivo de libro `Databases/ELEMENT4.ELE`.
2. IUPAC Commission on Isotopic Abundances and Atomic Weights (CIAAW) — *Standard Atomic Weights of the Elements*.
3. Haynes, W. M. (Ed.) — *CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics*, Section 4: Properties of the Elements and Inorganic Compounds.
4. NIST Atomic Spectra Database (SRD 78) — Ionization Energies & Ground-State Electronic Configurations.
5. Pauling, L. (1960) — *The Nature of the Chemical Bond*, 3rd ed., Cornell University Press (Allred–Rochow / Pauling Electronegativity Scale).
6. Shannon, R. D. (1976) — *Revised effective ionic radii and systematic studies of interatomic distances in halides and chalcogenides*, Acta Crystallogr. A32, 751–767.
7. Emsley, J. (2011) — *Nature's Building Blocks: An A–Z Guide to the Elements*, Oxford University Press.
8. Wagman, D. D. et al. (1982) — *The NBS Tables of Chemical Thermodynamic Properties* (datos estándar de elementos gaseosos/sólidos a `298.15 K`).
